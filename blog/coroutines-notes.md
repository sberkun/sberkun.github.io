
In the beginning, there was the program; a "routine" of instructions that the computer shall run to make the humans happy.

But the program was chaotic and messy and full of `GOTO`, and this was not good. So humans created the subroutine, which let a "routine" of instructions invoke smaller routines to do their bidding.
